# Limits of these results

Ordered by how substantially lifting each limitation could change the conclusions;
this is a review priority, not an empirically estimated effect ranking.

1. **Generated workloads only.** Supplied finite programs determine which paid
   work occurs; the measurements do not establish human conditioning, natural
   demand, learning, or responses to arbitrary social situations.
2. **Wide provenance over-counts semantic delivery.** Generic operation inputs,
   including invitations, assign supplier tuples to every paid block. Wide gives
   an upper bound. Narrow excludes input.N and finds no cross-actor received uses
   in these histories, but still does not verify the lineage of each block's
   element. The positive result depends on the wide definition.
3. **The requested triad was not producible.** The authorized fixture can vary
   Alice's TIM but fixes Bob and Eve as SLI. Changing collective membership cannot
   create a LIE third member. The experiment therefore cannot identify an added
   person's presence effect or compare the requested pair with that triad.
4. **U11 directs work to particular seats.** U11 drives Bob mainly to strong
   seats: 40 of his 52 uses are strong, while Alice=IEE has 84 weak uses out of 85.
   Seat distributions depend on actor and supplied program; neither composition
   samples the full space of possible weak-seat demands. The matched coverage
   values do not establish general relation independence.
5. **Retention is nearly saturated in the wide received sample.** Repeated
   program steps produce hold values 43/45, 48/52 and 71/75, leaving little
   variation. Kept is a later same-seat repeat, not retained information or
   acquired capability. Narrow hold is undefined because no uses are received.
6. **Four delivery seeds vary only nonbinding budgets.** They produce different
   checkpoint bytes but the same work program and measured profiles, so they
   do not establish robustness across independent workloads or random trials.
7. **Eve is inactive in every delivery history.** Her committed weak seats stay
   in the coverage denominator. These are two-participant collectives in worlds
   with three committed profiles, not two-profile worlds. Her sterile pairs and
   empty DCNH agreement cannot establish properties of her relation or type.
8. **Hop and content are counted alike by the Batch 7 indices.** Batch 8 reports
   them separately; Bob's weak-seat conditioning is entirely hops, and every
   route passes through a vital-ring support seat by construction. Which class
   should feed a DCNH load or a conditioning denominator is a theory question
   the companion does not decide.
9. **Observed supply does not establish lineage.** It requires only that the
   supplier completed a content block at the element earlier; it does not
   show that the consumed revision carried that element's content.
10. **Block energy is CT Table 13**, the grade-v1 debit, reported under every
   resource model; it is not the legacy-r4 wallet debit.
11. **pair-solo8 is one program on one actor.** Its collective phase starts from
   a different active element than the pair history (Alice's cursor after the
   uptake probe), so only the first operation's hops differ. Share and
   Coordinate rows need Bob as respondent; their uses are received. The
   workflow world's care stock allows two care rows. Seeds vary a nonbinding
   budget. Internalisation counts a later self/world use at the same seat of
   either class and shows no retained information.
12. **No historical archives were used.** Everything measured here is reproduced
   from the public pinned engine and the generated manifests; there is no
   comparison with private archival journals or independent empirical data.

The companion adds no feedback, regeneration, practice pricing, subtype assignment,
Model G seats, or new demand formation. DCNH greatest-load cells are descriptions,
not diagnoses. A zero received-kept vector ties every cell. Engine hashes establish
source identity, not theory validity. Relations name the measured combinations;
they do not cause or select uses.
